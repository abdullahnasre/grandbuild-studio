Our Story

Twenty-five years of building trust.

Meridian Construction Group was founded in 2001 in Munich with a single conviction: every landmark starts with a promise kept. Today, we deliver commercial, industrial, residential and infrastructure projects across Germany and Central Europe.

Mission

Build enduring infrastructure that elevates communities and creates lasting value for clients, partners and generations.

Vision

To be the most trusted construction partner across Germany — the standard against which the industry measures itself.

Core Values

Integrity. Safety. Precision. Accountability. Craft. These five principles govern every decision we make.

Milestones

A timeline of signature moments.
